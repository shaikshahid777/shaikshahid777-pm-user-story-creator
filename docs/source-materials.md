# Source Materials

The ChatGPT Project uses three supporting documents to reinforce product-ticketing consistency.

## 01 — Jira User Story Template & Formatting Guide
Standard user-story format, acceptance criteria, edge cases, priority, Markdown, and Jira/Linear-ready formatting.

File: `sources/01_Jira_User_Story_Template_Guide.docx`

## 02 — Product Ticketing Conventions
Scope control, product-level language, technical leakage prevention, and quality checks.

File: `sources/02_Product_Ticketing_Conventions.docx`

## 03 — Agile Acceptance Criteria & Edge Cases Guide
Testable acceptance criteria, observable outcomes, realistic edge cases, expected responses, and minimum quality bar.

File: `sources/03_Agile_Acceptance_Criteria_Edge_Cases_Guide.docx`

## Source-use model

These are supporting references. Their purpose is to reinforce ticket structure and quality; they do not expand feature scope.
